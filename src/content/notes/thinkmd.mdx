<Lead>ThinkMD’s digital health platform empowers health workers of all skill levels to assess patients and determine appropriate treatment steps, even in offline settings. However, the existing UX/UI was outdated and needed a significant upgrade to improve accessibility, user engagement, and visual clarity.</Lead>

Our team reimagined ThinkMD’s user experience with a modern, intuitive interface that ensures seamless navigation for users across diverse healthcare environments. We integrated universally recognizable 3D medical illustrations and animations to improve comprehension, particularly for health workers with limited formal training.

<Figure media="notes/thinkmd/think-md-mobile-app-v4.mp4" caption="01 / The redesigned mobile app" dark />

The redesigned app balances clinical accuracy with a user-friendly design, enhancing decision-making while maintaining the scientifically validated core functionality that sets ThinkMD apart. The result is a highly adaptable, visually engaging digital health tool that supports frontline healthcare workers worldwide.

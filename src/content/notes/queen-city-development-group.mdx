<Lead>When Queen City Development Group approached us, they needed more than a website. They needed a brand identity that reflected their expertise in construction, design & development, investment and property management.</Lead>

The brief covered three things: a new logo that captured their values and industry, brand guidelines to keep everything consistent, and a modern website to showcase their services and portfolio. All of it had to speak to investors, property owners and prospective clients alike, and feel professional without being stiff.

## The brand

We started with the logo and brand guidelines before moving on to the website. The mark pairs a four-leaf clover set in a circle with a sturdy geometric wordmark. The guidelines set out a palette of navy, cream, orange and rust, a type system of Proxima Nova, DM Sans and DM Mono, and a tone of voice, giving every piece of marketing and communication the same foundation.

## The website

The site carries the new identity through with clean layouts and a warm, neutral palette that conveys expertise and trust. Visitors can explore each of the four services in turn, and a portfolio section showcases completed projects with an emphasis on scope, quality and attention to detail. Testimonials, case studies and an About Us section build credibility.

<Figure media="notes/queen-city-development-group/website-mockup.jpg" caption="02 / The new homepage" />

It’s fully responsive, fast-loading and built on a custom WordPress theme following SEO best practices, and it scores 100 across the board in Lighthouse.

## The results

Together, the logo, brand guidelines and website position Queen City Development Group as a leader in their industry. Their online presence now shows the same quality and attention to detail as their projects, building credibility and bringing in new business.

## Services provided

- Logo design
- Brand guidelines, including typography, color palette and tone of voice
- Website design, from the homepage through the services, projects and about pages
- Custom WordPress theme development, optimized for performance and search engines

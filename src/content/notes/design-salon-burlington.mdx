<Lead>We’re throwing a little design get-together in Burlington tonight, along with our pal Katie McCurdy and the fine folks at Frank.</Lead>

Vermont is filled with dynamic and talented people working in digital design, but many of us haven’t had the opportunity to get to know one another. Let’s change that.

Please come if you’re a UX designer, product designer, interaction designer, UI designer, or you work on interactive digital stuff.

During this casual, chill, yet structured evening in VCET’s stylish Barn Room, we will:

- Do a drawing exercise to get to know each other
- Hear briefly from three design peers: [Claire Holloway](https://www.linkedin.com/in/claire-holloway) of [Fluency](https://www.fluency.inc/), [James Kowalski](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-kowalski-3029a55/) of [Simple Creature](https://www.simplecreature.us/), and [Stacy Kellner](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stacy-kellner-09995093/) of [Bauer](https://www.bauer.com/) will share a little about their work
- Consume light beverages and snacks

The RSVP is closed now, but I think there are a few spots left. Hit me up if you like design and cool people.

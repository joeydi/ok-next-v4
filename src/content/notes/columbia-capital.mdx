<Lead>Columbia Capital is a leading investment firm that helps entrepreneurs build successful businesses. Their website was looking dated and wasn’t communicating their achievements or services.</Lead>

Our goal was to create a website that reflected their position as industry leaders and showcased their success stories.

<Figure media="notes/columbia-capital/columbia-capital-website.mp4" caption="01 / The new homepage" />

## Branding

We started with design exploration to update the visuals and make sure they aligned with the company’s image and values: a refined wordmark, a bold geometric type pairing, and three accent colors set against Columbia’s navy.

## The website

General design updates modernized the look and feel of the whole site, including the header, footer, typography and other global elements. A new homepage brings together content from the old Home, About, and Strategy pages to give a clear, concise overview of the company. The Team, Portfolio, Sector, and Contact pages were redesigned to better showcase the firm’s expertise.

## Services provided

- Design exploration and branding
- General website design updates, including the header, footer, typography and other global elements
- New homepage design combining content from the existing Home, About, and Strategy pages
- Updated designs for the Team, Portfolio, Sector, and Contact pages
- Front and back-end development, with a seamless user experience and optimization for search engines

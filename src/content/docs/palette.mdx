The tokens are the `--color-*` variables in the `@theme` block of `src/app/globals.css`, and every value on this page is read from there. Use them as Tailwind classes (`bg-paper`, `text-muted`) or as `var(--color-…)`, never as a hex.

## Swatches

Each swatch shows its best contrast, against paper or ink, and copies its hex when clicked.

## HSL

The neutrals are set from one rule rather than picked by eye, so a new one can be placed by its lightness alone. Hover a dot for its token and value.

The steps are the `--spacing-fl-*` variables and the gutter `--spacing-gutter` in `src/app/fluid.css`, which `scripts/fluid.mjs` generates along with the page's max width, `--container-page`: edit the script and rerun it, never the CSS. The frame's utilities (`px-page`, `grid-12`) and `--pvw` are in `src/app/globals.css`. Every value on this page is read from those files. Use the steps with any spacing utility (`p-fl-24`, `gap-y-fl-40`, `mt-fl-96`) rather than Tailwind's fixed steps or px.

## Scale

Each step is named for its px at 1440 and is a straight line from a smaller px at a 320 window, carried on to 1920 and clamped at both ends, the same as the type sizes. The large steps shrink to about half on a phone and the small ones to about three quarters, so sections close up more than the gaps inside them. The gutter is the one named step: 64 at 1440 like `fl-64`, but down to 20 on a phone, so narrow screens keep more of their width for content.

## Page

Every page-level block sits in `<Container>` (`src/components/Container.tsx`): `px-page` sets the gutter either side, and `max-w-page` caps it at `--container-page` and centres it. That's where the fluid scale stops growing too, so past 1920 the page just gains margins. Full-bleed backgrounds, like the footer's, go on a parent with the container inside.

Inside it, `grid-12` lays blocks out on twelve columns with `fl-24` between them. Place things with `col-span-*` and `col-start-*`; most blocks span all twelve on a phone and split from `lg`. The note article (`.note-body`) is the same grid.

Two more variables in `globals.css` measure off the frame:

- `--pvw` is `1vw` that stops growing at `--container-page`. For widths and offsets taken from the comp as a share of the window, write `calc(N*var(--pvw))` rather than `Nvw`, so they stop at the cap along with everything else. The heroes' illustrations are placed this way.
- `--nav-h` is the sticky nav's height, from its padding steps below `lg` and fixed from there. `scroll-padding-top` uses it, so anchor jumps land below the nav.

## Spacing in use

Every padding, margin, gap and offset set in `src/app`, `src/components` and `globals.css`, found by scanning the source, that isn't a step on the scale. Fixed px stays the same size at every width, which is right for controls that shouldn't shrink on a phone, such as the nav's hit areas, the skip link and the booking dialog's close button. Elsewhere, a fixed value is a candidate for the nearest step, marked in pink. Em and % values scale with their text or box, and worked-out values combine tokens, so they're only listed, in the table at the end.
